# Alternative C - 3D concept model (design Rev 3, bases Rev 4b)

3D model of the post-and-beam braced steel roof over the existing slab, built for the client to understand the column / beam / bracing system. Everything is generated by `build_3d.py` from the design geometry - nothing is drawn by hand and no member is invented:

- `design/C/calc/model.py` - column nodes (27 concrete columns from `geometry.json`), the 11 rafter lines, the 20 primaries / eave beams / trimmer T2, the 10 braced bays B1-B10, the north-edge jog, `TOS(y) = 3.33 + 0.06 (35.87 - y)`, column tops TOS - 0.30, primary tops TOS + 0.05.
- `design/C/calc/bracing.py` - the 24 roof rod panels (RT-N-W, RT-N-E, RT-S-E, RT-S-W, RT-W, RT-E, RT-JOG).
- `detailing/gen/geom.py` (the drawing generator, cross-checked against model.py at run time) - purlin rows, roof-truss posts ST1 / ST2, WP1 at its Rev 4b position, base types B1 / B2 / P, girt rows, eave rails and the wall header.

Units m, world coordinates identical to the plan (S01) and `geometry.json`; z = 0 is the top of the existing slab. Regenerate with `cd roof/3d && python3 build_3d.py` (ezdxf 1.4, matplotlib). `model_stats.json` holds the entity counts and the source cross-checks.

## Viewing the DXF

The model-space viewport is saved as a **SW isometric, flat shaded with edges** (VPORT direction -1,-1,1, target at the building centre), so AutoCAD / BricsCAD / DWG TrueView open it in 3D. Four named views are stored: `SW-ISO`, `SE-ISO`, `NE-ISO`, `NW-ISO` (`VIEW` > Restore). If the viewer ignores the saved viewport, type `VPOINT` and enter `-1,-1,1` (SW) or `1,-1,1` (SE), then `SHADE` (or `VSCURRENT` > Conceptual); `-VIEW` `_SWISO` also works. Freeze `3D-PANEL` to look at the framing, freeze `3D-PURL` as well to see the roof rods, isolate `3D-BRACE-WALL` + `3D-BRACE-ROOF` + `3D-COL` for the lateral system alone. All bodies are MESH entities (metre units, `$INSUNITS = 6`), so they also import into Rhino, SketchUp, Navisworks and Blender.

## How the system works (plain language)

**Gravity.** Loads land on the 50 mm sandwich panel, which spans 1.5 m between the Z200 purlins. The purlins carry them to the green IPE 270 rafters (2-3 m apart), the rafters to the red IPE 330 primaries running east-west on every column row, and the primaries down through the 27 blue HEA 160 columns into the existing concrete columns under the slab. Every joint is a simple pin; nothing relies on frame action.

**Lateral (wind, seismic).** Wind on the wall panels goes into the girts, which span column to column; each column sends half of its wall load to its base and half up to the roof. There the yellow M24 rod panels turn the roof into a stiff plate (diaphragm) that carries the load sideways to the ten magenta X-braced wall bays B1-B10, whose diagonals take it straight down to the base plates, shear keys and anchors in the slab. Those ten bays must stay door-free.

**Level primaries, sloping rafters.** The primaries sit level on the column cap plates, so every column and cap detail is the same; the 6 % drainage fall is made by the rafters alone, whose tops follow the plane from 4.55 m (south) to 3.33 m (north).

**Openings.** The wells are not roofed; trimmer T2 and the rafters beside the wells give the purlins and the upstand an edge to bear on. The stair well reaches the north wall, so a wall header replaces the eave beam there.

**Thermal.** East-west movement is restrained by the path B2 - roof rods - B1 and designed for; elsewhere the fin-plate clearances release it.

## Mismatches found between model.py and the drawings

- WP1 is at the notch corner (77.89, 19.97) in `model.py` (wall-load model) but at (77.61, 20.25) on S01 / S02 (Rev 4b, moved 280 mm inboard); the drawn position is used here.
- Rafter lines are not on the column centres on lines 3, 5, 6 and 11: R72 is 100 mm east of K26 (71.99), R78 110 mm west of K10 (77.89), R82 (x 81.85) is 40-70 mm off K3, K11, K17, K21 and R95 (x 95.55) 40-70 mm off K4, K14, K18, K23. The drawings accept this ("columns at true positions") because the rafters bear on the primaries, not on the columns; the model shows the rafters where the design puts them.
- `model.py`'s docstring still describes Rev 2 levels (TOS 3.30, column length TOS - 0.34) while its functions and the drawings are Rev 3 (3.33, TOS - 0.35); the functions are used.
- The rafter / primary / bay / rod-panel lists in `model.py`, `bracing.py` and `detailing/gen/geom.py` agree exactly (checked at run time).
